# Westline Carriers – TPMS Fleet Analysis

An evidence-based Data Analytics and Business Analysis investigation of tyre pressure monitoring and fleet tyre performance.

## Overview

This project examines tyre-related operational concerns within the Westline Carriers fleet using Tyre Pressure Monitoring System (TPMS) exports and supporting operational records.

The investigation focuses on understanding tyre pressure and temperature behaviour, examining reported incidents, reviewing vehicle and inspection information, and identifying data-quality issues that may affect the interpretation of results.

The objective is to present findings supported by the available evidence while clearly distinguishing observations, interpretations and unresolved questions.

## Areas Investigated

- Tyre pressure and temperature behaviour.
- Abnormal pressure changes and individual tyre observations.
- The reported T2 tyre incident on 11 July 2026.
- Differences in depot inspection practices.
- Vehicle-level and dual-tyre pressure observations.
- Tread-depth inspection coverage and sensor battery indicators.
- Data inconsistencies, missing information and source reliability.
- Operational information required for further investigation.

## Approach

1. Reviewed the supplied assessment documents, data dictionary and tyre inflation specifications.
2. Examined the vehicle register, inspection records and daily distance information.
3. Organised and prepared relevant data using Microsoft Excel.
4. Examined TPMS readings and compared observations with the documented specifications.
5. Cross-checked information across the available sources.
6. Documented findings, data limitations and areas requiring further verification.
7. Prepared the investigation report and presentation.

## Deliverables

- **HTML Investigation Report** – Detailed findings, charts, data limitations and recommended next steps.
- **Findings Presentation** – Summary of the most important findings.
- **Fleet Manager Questions** – Five prioritised questions addressing information gaps.
- **Data Distrust Memo** – Identified data-quality concerns and their impact on the investigation.

## Important Limitations

The available data does not establish every physical cause of tyre-pressure changes or confirm whether TPMS alerts were delivered and acknowledged.

Incomplete inspection records, differences in measurement routines and the absence of certain operational information also limit some comparisons.

No predictive failure capability is claimed on the basis of this investigation.
